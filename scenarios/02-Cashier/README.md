# 💵 Scenario: Cashier Threat Hunting Lab

## 📖 Mission briefing Background Information 
Manager Karen claims she was locked in her secure secure admin office doing paperwork, but vault logs trace back to the noisy staff breakroom. 

## 🔍 Network Baseline & Expectations
* **Admin Office:** '192.168.10.1'
* **Staff breakroom:** '192.168.10.25'
* **Registers:** '192.168.10.x'
* **Expected Behavior:** Cashiers log into their assigned point of sale registers during store operated hours, managers perform register overrides and administrative tasks from the secure admin office terminal, Safe or vault access is handled strictly within secure backoffice-zones.

* **cashier_beginner.py :** Level 1 Subtle anomalies
* **cashier_intermediate.py :**  Level 2 Multi step threats
* **cashier_advanced.py :** Level 3 Complex cyberattacks
